Led a team designing a <strong>Laptop Cooler</strong> with enhanced, user-centric features through <strong>Product Design Lab I &amp; II</strong> at RUET—from pain-point research and conceptual design through survey analysis, material selection, stress analysis, and cost analysis. The build uses an <strong>Arduino Nano</strong> microcontroller, <strong>LM-35DZ</strong> temperature sensing, dual adaptive <strong>12V fans</strong>, and an <strong>SPI display</strong>, with ergonomic support including anti-slide guard, adjustable arms, cable organizer, and mobile holder.
